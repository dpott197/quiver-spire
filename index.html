    Controls

    ←→ or AD
    Run
    ↑↓ or WS
    Aim up or down. Down drops through ledges
    Z J Space
    Jump. Press again in mid-air to double-jump, or against a wall to wall-jump
    X K
    Shoot in the held direction (8-way)
    C L Shift
    Dodge. Catches arrows mid-dash. Jump during a dodge on the ground for a super jump that keeps the dash speed
    Esc P
    Pause (online, only the host can pause)
    Controller
    Stick or d-pad to move and aim, A jump, X or Y shoot, B or any bumper or trigger dodge, Start to begin or pause

    Your own arrows can't hurt you. Land on a head to stomp. Walk over arrows to collect them, up to six. A round ends after 90 seconds if no one wins it. Online, the host's browser runs the match, so the host should keep this page open.

    Treasure

    Chests drop into the arena during each round. Touch one to open it. Power-ups last until the round ends.

    Bomb arrows
    Two arrows that explode on impact
    Laser arrows
    Two fast arrows that fly straight and bounce off walls
    Bramble arrows
    Two arrows that leave thorns rivals can't touch
    Drill arrows
    Two arrows that bore through one block
    Shield
    Blocks the next hit
    Wings
    Flap as often as you like in mid-air
